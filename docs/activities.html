CECAM · RECURRING ACTIVITIES

Year-round series, in parallel with the workshops.

Beyond the funded program, CECAM runs six evergreen series — lectures, conversations, archives and a prize — that keep the community in touch between workshops.

01 / Online

§ 01 · Online talks & posters

Mixed-Gen.

A series of online talks and poster sessions on advanced topics in simulation and modelling, and their applications — designed for simulators young in years or at heart. The format mixes generations on purpose: PhD students present alongside senior researchers, with discussion the central activity.

FORMATOnline · talks + posters RHYTHMSeveral sessions per year
02 / Lecture series

§ 02 · Invited lectures since 2015

CECAM Lectures.

In April 2015 CECAM initiated a series of lectures to highlight interesting developments across computational science. Speakers are invited by the director's office; each lecture is recorded and made public so that the archive itself becomes a methodological reference for the community.

SINCEApril 2015 FORMATSingle-lecture, recorded ARCHIVEOnline
03 / Methods archive

§ 03 · CECAM–MARVEL co-production

MARVEL Classics.

A different look at fundamental developments of simulation and modelling. Milestone conceptual steps, methods and algorithms are presented by their originators — the people who first wrote the paper, prototyped the code, or named the technique. Co-produced with the MARVEL National Centre of Competence in Research.

FORMATRecorded interviews PARTNERNCCR MARVEL
04 / Conversation series

§ 04 · Beyond the methods

Mary Ann Mansigh Conversation Series.

The Mary Ann Mansigh Conversation Series focuses on non-strictly-technical topics of cultural interest to the simulation and modelling community: ethics, the history of the field, the politics of open science, what computers were like when our citations were footnotes. Talks introduce the topic; the central event is the conversation that follows.

FORMATTalk + audience conversation TONEInformal & informative
05 / Short online talks

§ 05 · Bite-sized methods

CECAM Webinars.

Short online talks on simulation methods, hosted across the network and archived afterwards. The webinar format complements the in-person workshops with a steady drumbeat of methodological updates from researchers across the twenty-two nodes.

FORMATOnline · 45 min + Q&A ARCHIVERecorded

§ 06 · Award

Berni J. Alder CECAM Prize.

The Berni J. Alder CECAM Prize recognises exceptional contributions to the field of microscopic simulation of matter. Named for one of the founders of molecular dynamics, the prize is awarded by CECAM in collaboration with the European Physical Society's Computational Physics group.

NAMED FORBerni J. Alder FIELDMicroscopic simulation of matter WITHEPS Computational Physics
