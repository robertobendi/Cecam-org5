CECAM · ABOUT

Fifty years of European computational science.

CECAM is a non-profit European research network, anchored at EPFL Lausanne since 2003, that promotes fundamental research in computational atomic, molecular and materials simulation across a federation of twenty-two nodes.

§ 01 · Mission

CECAM exists to promote fundamental research on advanced computational methods, and to apply them to the most consequential problems at the frontiers of science and technology. As the name suggests, the traditional focus has been atomistic and molecular simulation, applied to the physics and chemistry of condensed matter.

Over the last twenty years, powerful advances in hardware and software have extended these methods to a wide range of problems in materials science, biology and medicinal chemistry. The role of CECAM is to curate the workshops, schools, conferences and series that help the community keep up with its own pace of change — peer-led, never commercial, always for the community.

The federated structure is intentional. Twenty-two nodes across Europe and beyond host their own programmes; the headquarters at EPFL coordinates, funds the flagship calls, and protects the institutional memory of a network that turned fifty in 2019.

Governance

Director, Council, and Scientific Advisory Committee.

3 bodies

Director

Office of the Director

Responsible for the overall scientific program, the flagship call, and CECAM's relationship with EPFL and member institutions.

Council

Member-institution Council

Representatives of CECAM's member institutions across Europe — sets policy, oversees finances, approves the flagship program.

Advisory

Scientific Advisory Committee

Rotating committee of senior researchers that reviews the flagship call and advises on the strategic direction of the network.

History

From Orsay to EPFL — half a century in five steps.

1969
Founded in Orsay

CECAM is founded outside Paris as a centre for atomic and molecular calculations — one of the earliest dedicated facilities of its kind in Europe.

1992
Workshop model takes hold

The peer-proposed, peer-organised workshop becomes the centre's defining format — small intensive meetings where the program is the science.

2003
Move to EPFL Lausanne

CECAM-HQ relocates to EPFL in Lausanne, becoming the central node of a federated European network of member institutions.

2015
CECAM Lectures launch

An online lecture series begins, broadening reach beyond in-person workshops and creating a public archive of methodological talks.

2019
Fiftieth anniversary

CECAM marks fifty years of European computational science with a plenary at the SwissTech Convention Center, Lausanne.

2026
Flagship 2027–2028 call

The next two-year flagship call opens, shaping the program of workshops, schools and conferences across the network for 2027–2028.
