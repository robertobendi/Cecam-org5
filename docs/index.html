About CECAM

A federated European network, fifty years in.

CECAM is an organisation devoted to fundamental research on advanced computational methods and their application to frontier problems in physics, chemistry, materials, biology and medicinal chemistry. Over the last twenty years powerful advances in hardware and software have extended atomistic and molecular simulation across the sciences — CECAM curates the program of workshops, schools and conferences that helps the community keep up.
