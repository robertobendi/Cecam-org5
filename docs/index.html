§ 05 · About

A federated European network — fifty years in.

Read our story →

CECAM curates the program of workshops, schools and conferences that helps the community keep up with computational science.

An organisation devoted to fundamental research on advanced computational methods and their application to frontier problems in physics, chemistry, materials, biology and medicinal chemistry. Over the last twenty years powerful advances in hardware and software have extended atomistic and molecular simulation across the sciences — CECAM convenes the peer-led program that helps the European community stay at the frontier.

Charter · since 1969 · Lausanne
