17 MAR 2026 · LORENTZ SELECTION

The 2027 CECAM-Lorentz workshop has been selected: Fracture of Amorphous Materials Across the Scales.

Congratulations to Maximilian Ries, Sebastian Pfaller and Andrea Giuntoli. The workshop will take place at CECAM-HQ, EPFL, Lausanne on 12–16 April 2027 and will bring together computational and experimental researchers working on damage and failure of glasses and amorphous polymers, with sessions on multiscale modelling, machine-learning interatomic potentials, and the interpretation of fracture surfaces at the atomic scale.
